The English and Russian next-word tables (en.tsv, ru.tsv) and common-word lists
(en-words.txt, ru-words.txt) in this folder are built by mobile/scripts/build-next-words.ts
from sentences published by Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org), licensed CC BY 2.0 FR
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/). Only words and counts of which word
follows which are kept; no sentence is included.
